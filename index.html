    Help & FAQ

    Can I add unlimited subjects?

    Yes. Use "Add Subject" in either the Marks Calculator or the Grade-Based Calculator as many times as you need — there's no fixed limit.

    Can I create my own grading system?

    Yes. Open "Edit Grading Scale" to add, edit, or remove grades, change their percentage ranges, and mark which ones count as passing.

    Are my marks stored online?

    No. Everything — your subjects, marks, and grading scale — is saved only in your browser's local storage on this device. Nothing is sent to a server.

    Can I use this calculator for different schools and universities?

    Yes. Since the grading scale is fully editable, you can set it up to match any school, college, or university system.

    Grading systems vary widely between institutions, and even between departments at the same institution. This calculator is a general-purpose tool, not an official record. If your result will be used for admissions, transcripts, transfers, or any other official purpose, always confirm the exact grading rules with your school or university directly.

    About

    Student Marks & Grade Calculator is a free tool for converting between percentages and grades, calculating marks across multiple subjects, and working out grade-based results — all using a grading scale you can fully customize.

    What you can do here

    • Quick Convert — look up the grade for a single percentage.
    • Marks Calculator — add subjects with marks and maximum marks to get per-subject and overall percentages, grades, and pass/fail status.
    • Grade-Based Calculator — enter a grade per subject to see the grade distribution and, where mathematically valid, a combined result.
    • Editable Grading Scale — the default scale is modeled on the Kerala Board system, but every grade, range, and pass/fail flag can be edited to match your own school, college, or university.

    Why it works this way

    Letter grades represent a range of percentages, not one exact number. Rather than guessing a single percentage for a grade, this tool always shows the true range, and is upfront when a combined result genuinely can't be determined from the information given.

    Who it's for

    Students, parents, and teachers who want a quick, private way to work out results without creating an account or sending data anywhere. Everything you enter stays in your own browser.
